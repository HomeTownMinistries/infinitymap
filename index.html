Simple to Complete

Filling it in is the easy part

Your Infinity Map arrives as a fillable PDF the moment you check out. Complete it whichever way feels most natural.

💻

Type it on your computer

Open the PDF in the free Adobe Acrobat Reader (or Preview on a Mac), click any field to type, and save as you go. No account, no internet needed.

🖨️

Print it and write by hand

Print the whole workbook and fill it in with a pen — many families love a handwritten copy that becomes a keepsake in a loved one's own writing.

📍

Store it & tell someone

Keep the finished Map with your important documents, and tell at least one family member exactly where it is. A plan no one can find helps no one.

One more thing: your completed Infinity Map holds sensitive information — keep it on your own computer or on paper. Please don't email the finished copy to anyone.

Common Questions

Good questions, honest answers

Is the Infinity Map a will or a legal document?

No. The Infinity Map is a planning workbook — it organizes your information, wishes, and story in one place so your family isn't left searching. It is not legal advice and doesn't replace a will, living will, or power of attorney. We encourage you to bring your completed Map to an attorney; it makes preparing those legal documents faster and easier.

How do I receive my Infinity Map?

Instantly. The moment your checkout completes, you land on a download page with your fillable PDF. If you ever misplace it, email us and we'll send it to you directly.

Can my spouse and I both use one purchase?

Yes — your purchase covers your household. Save or print a copy for each family member; every person should complete their own Map.

Do you see what I fill in?

Never. The PDF lives on your computer or on paper — nothing you write in it is ever sent to us or stored online. Your information stays entirely in your hands.
